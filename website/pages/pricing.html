What counts as a node

Counts

Every machine that runs meshvpn in the network: devices, gateways, admin machines, GPU servers, the portal's instance.

Doesn't count

People and AI agents. They come in through the gateway with their SSH key and need nothing installed.

Grows with you

Add nodes during the year and pay pro rata. A license can cover several networks of your organization.

Questions

What happens when the license expires?

The last policy stays in force: bookings end as planned, devices stay locked down. You can't change users, rules or bookings until a new license is installed.

Does meshvpn enterprise send our data to PlugOvr?

No. Everything runs on your machines. A trial request sends an e-mail address, company name and network id to our license server. Updates come from GitHub.

Can we try it on our own hardware first?

Yes: the 30-day trial has all features for up to 25 nodes. Start it with sudo meshvpn license trial --email you@example.com --accept-terms or the portal's button.

How do we pay?

By invoice, yearly in advance. Prices are net, plus VAT where it applies. Within the EU, businesses with a VAT number get reverse-charge invoices.

Which terms apply?

The license terms. You accept them when you start a trial or install a license; the network's audit log records who accepted which version and when.

Is the open-source version limited?

No. The mesh VPN and all its features are free, without node or time limits. The enterprise edition adds bookings, the central policy, gateways, the portal and the audit log.
